# Conflict as a Bug — v0.1

## Goal

Help two people reach confirmed mutual understanding of their perspectives.

## Direct conversation

Direct conversation is the preferred path for A and B. A recommended way to hold it is to use a heart and a shell:

1. One person holds a heart and speaks with care.
2. The other person holds a shell and listens with attention and silence.
3. They exchange the objects and repeat the roles.

This practice is a recommendation, not a requirement.

## Alternative application path

The application offers an alternative when direct conversation is unfeasible, inappropriate, interrupted, or insufficient. It begins when A prepares an invitation.

## Application flow

1. A writes `How I see it` and prepares the invitation.
2. B receives A’s contribution and writes `How I see it`.
3. Both contributions are free-form perspectives.
4. A paraphrases B, and B paraphrases A.
5. Each person confirms the paraphrase or adds a clarification.
6. The paraphrases can be revised until both are confirmed.
7. Confirmed mutual understanding is the implemented private-flow endpoint. A separate `What I’m asking for now` field remains a product-design idea, not an implemented v0.1 stage.

8. If both choose to open the case to solvers, each authenticates by email and signs with a Privy embedded wallet. Login and signing are intentionally separate clicks.
9. The first signature creates and fixes an encrypted `openEnvelope`, records the first consent, and produces a new encrypted transport link. The second signature is over the same envelope and opens the case only if it comes from a different address.
10. The browser then sends the plain-text perspectives/paraphrases separately for summarization; the showcase displays the opened case, summary and solver contributions.

## Scope

Confirmed understanding remains different from agreement or resolution.

Version 0.1 begins with one private case and two people. Opening that case and accepting unmoderated solver contributions in the showcase are implemented; solver selection, resolution mechanisms, incentives, payments and NFTs are not part of v0.1.

The private link is a bearer secret: its fragment carries the decryption key, so it must stay in the chosen channel. The chain receives only hashes, signatures, addresses and state; it never receives the conflict text. The public summary is generated from plain text sent separately to the backend after opening.
