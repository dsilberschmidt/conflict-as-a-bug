# Conflict as a Bug — v0.1

## Goal

Help two people reach confirmed mutual understanding of their perspectives.

## Preliminary phase

Before using the application, the two people meet face to face. This phase is preliminary and will be expanded later.

1. One person holds a heart and speaks with care.
2. The other person holds a shell and listens with attention and silence.
3. They exchange the objects and repeat the roles.

## Application flow

The application begins when A prepares an invitation.

1. A writes `How I see it` and prepares the invitation.
2. B receives A’s contribution and writes `How I see it`.
3. Both contributions are free-form perspectives.
4. A paraphrases B, and B paraphrases A.
5. Each person confirms the paraphrase or adds a clarification.
6. The paraphrases can be revised until both are confirmed.
7. After confirmed mutual understanding, each person may add `What I’m asking for now`.

## Scope

Confirmed understanding remains different from agreement or resolution.

Version 0.1 is limited to one private case and two people.
